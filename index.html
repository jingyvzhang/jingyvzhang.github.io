I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Fudan University, expected to graduate in 2027. Prior to this, I received my BEng from Shandong University.

My research interests include multi-agent collaborative perception, multimodal safety alignment, large language model post-training, and autonomous driving.

In total, 15 papers published, including 11 CCF-A / CAS Q1 papers and 7 first-author papers across ICML, ICLR, CVPR, AAAI, ACM MM, TITS, and TVT. Two papers under review at TMC and TMM.
